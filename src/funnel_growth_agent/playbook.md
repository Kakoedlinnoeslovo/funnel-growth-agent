CRO playbook for landing → CTA only.

- Match hero wording to ad intent. If paid traffic promises JPG→SVG or “editable vector”, the first screen should say that.
- Keep the primary CTA visible in the first screen. Do not bury the first action.
- Avoid multiple competing actions above the fold.
- Bring proof closer to the first CTA when users drop early.
- Reduce unnecessary scroll before the primary action.
- Do not repeat patterns already present.
- Sticky CTA already exists on v7; do not “discover” it as a new idea.
- Do not repeat a failed experiment family without new evidence.
- Prefer one minimal hero_copy change over a rewrite of every section.
- Do not invent product claims the ads make if Recraft cannot support them.
- Do not change prices, plan names, card types, media sources, or checkout.
- V6 numbers are a proxy. Use them as evidence of current paid-social landing→CTA, not as v7’s measured rate.
- If the landing already restates the winning ad promise, decline. A forced experiment is worse than no_experiment.
