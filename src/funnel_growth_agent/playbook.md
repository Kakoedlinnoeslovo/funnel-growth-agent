CRO playbook for landing → CTA only.

- Match the first screen to ad intent. If paid traffic promises JPG→SVG or “editable vector”, the headline, the clip and the button should all say that.
- One coherent hypothesis, expressed everywhere the visitor meets the promise: headline, clip, every CTA label, and the order of proof. Half-measures test nothing.
- Keep the primary CTA visible in the first screen. Do not bury the first action.
- Avoid multiple competing actions above the fold.
- Match every CTA label on the page to the hero CTA so the promise never changes wording.
- Pick the clip that makes the promise legible without copy: the action starts within three seconds and the first frame already shows the result. Start at least 1 s into the source (never 0): product videos fade in from black and the poster is the first frame of the trim. video-first only when the clip carries the promise alone; otherwise copy-first with the clip under the button.
- Bring proof closer to the first CTA when users drop early: move logo-strip or press-quotes up.
- Omit sections that restate a promise the ads never made; two omissions at most.
- Showcase tiles: the visual family is the hypothesis. Read get_visual_landscape and choose the family the evidence points at: what the top paid ads look like (spend-weighted), what competitor first screens show, what our tiles already are, what was tested and how it did. Replace whole groups or nothing (3–4 slots), one family per group, one colour story per tile, one subject; a lettering tile carries only the quoted words; a before/after is one object split down its middle (or, in ugc, one person holding both), never two copies side by side; reference real tiles of the group plus `previous`. Do not repeat a family marked directionally_worse without new evidence; list the families you did not choose in otherIdeas with their evidence.
- Reduce unnecessary scroll before the primary action.
- Do not repeat patterns already present.
- Sticky CTA already exists on the base landing; do not “discover” it as a new idea.
- Do not repeat a failed experiment family without new evidence, and do not reuse a hero clip that a previous run marked directionally_worse.
- Do not invent product claims the ads make if Recraft cannot support them.
- Do not change prices, plan names, card types, or checkout.
- Older-version numbers (e.g. v6) are a proxy. Use them as evidence of current paid-social landing→CTA, not as the base version’s measured rate.
- If the landing already restates the winning ad promise in copy, clip and proof, decline. A forced experiment is worse than no_experiment.
